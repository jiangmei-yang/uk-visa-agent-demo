# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is an evaluator or operations reviewer inspecting how an automated UK
Standard Visitor document-preparation service behaves. The represented end user is an adult
applicant who has already chosen this route and is preparing evidence through email.

## Product Purpose

The product demonstrates one complete, auditable preparation loop: receive a synthetic email
thread and documents, preserve evidence provenance, identify blockers without making legal
claims, obtain corrections and explicit confirmation, and produce a reviewable application pack.

## Positioning

Human-feeling guidance with a deterministic control plane: language can be flexible, but scope,
requirements, state changes, and delivery readiness cannot be improvised by a model.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, and accountable. The voice is considerate without sounding casual, and transparent
about uncertainty without becoming alarmist.

## Anti-references

This must not resemble a generic chatbot, a decorative AI dashboard, a government-service clone,
or a legal eligibility checker. Avoid fake confidence scores, approval predictions, dense warning
banners, and status displays that rely on colour alone.

## Design Principles

- Put evidence and blockers before generated prose.
- Make the deterministic gate inspectable at a glance.
- Distinguish synthetic, confirmed, unresolved, and stale data explicitly.
- Keep the interface dense enough for review work while preserving a clear reading order.
- Treat every finalisation action as consequential and show why it is or is not available.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-visible focus, semantic landmarks and tables, 4.5:1 text contrast,
non-colour state cues, responsive layouts down to 375px, and reduced-motion support.
